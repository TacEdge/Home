# HOME V0.1 — Scope

Status: **Direction approved; rev 2 amendments awaiting review.**

## 1. The one question

> **Does having Kev understand our family context make everyday family coordination meaningfully easier?**

Everything in V0.1 exists to answer that question with real use, by both of us, for a few weeks. Anything that doesn't help answer it is out.

## 2. How we got here

The original hypothesis was: *Today, Forward, Home Projects, Kev — with family members, events, commitments, simple tasks, home projects, notes/photos and Kev contextual awareness.*

Rev 1 adjusted it:
- **Read-only calendar sync in V0.1** — manual entry would leave HOME incomplete and Kev wrong.
- **Photos moved to V0.2.**
- **Week Ahead on demand added.**

Rev 2 (approved direction, amendments):
- **Calendar sync behind a provider interface** — ICS is the first adapter, not the architecture.
- **Model routing** — a fast tier for everyday questions and triage, a deep tier for planning.
- **Lightweight family profiles** — Kev understands the people behind the calendar.
- **Capture / To sort** — capture first, organise second.
- **Channel-agnostic Kev** — voice-ready, not voice-built.
- **Context instead of Fact** — family knowledge is dated, sourced, confirmable and can go stale.
- **The trust model** made a permanent principle.

## 3. V0.1 definition

### Screens
| Screen | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Date and a one-line weather note · each person's day (events, who's doing drop-off/pickup) · conflicts, if any · tasks due or scheduled today · anything Kev has noticed · a quiet "To sort (n)" line when captures are waiting · the Kev input. Fits on one phone screen in the common case. |
| **Forward** | 7 / 30 / 90-day toggle · grouped by week · events, birthdays, deadlines, scheduled tasks, project target dates · conflicts highlighted quietly · **Week Ahead** button. |
| **Projects** | Home projects (idea / active / paused / done) · project page: summary, tasks (estimate + needs), notes. |
| **People** | Reached by tapping a person. Lightweight profile: age/stage, regular week, things to know, coming up. |
| **Kev** | One input, available everywhere, for both asking and telling. Streaming answers led by a short sentence, tappable citations, capture confirmations, inline proposal cards (Approve / Edit / Reject). |
| **To sort** | Captures waiting to be organised, each with Kev's suggestion. Not a primary tab. |
| **Settings** | People · Calendars · *What Kev knows* (context, with stale items to confirm) · Activity (audit) · Usage · Export. |

The **screen concepts for Today, Forward and Kev** are designed and agreed (milestone M0) before any UI is built.

### Capabilities
- Two adult users, magic-link sign-in, allowlist.
- People with lightweight profiles: role, relationship, date of birth → age/stage, regular week (from recurring events), context. Children are profiles, not users.
- Events: manual (with recurrence) + read-only synced via the calendar provider interface, ICS adapter first; person/responsibility annotations.
- Tasks, home projects, notes.
- **Capture**: anything told to Kev is saved verbatim and privately first; Kev proposes structure; unsorted captures wait in To sort. Capture works even if Kev is unavailable.
- **Context**: Kev-proposed or user-added; subject, category, source, last confirmed, optional valid-until, sensitivity, visibility, status; staleness-aware.
- Deterministic engines: agenda, conflicts, free windows (daylight + weather), stage, staleness.
- Weather for home location.
- **Kev**: channel-agnostic orchestrator; fast/deep model routing with escalation and usage logging; read tools, `capture`, proposal tools.
- Week Ahead on demand (deep tier).
- Visibility (household/private) and sensitivity (normal/sensitive); conversations private per user.
- Audit log, usage log with spend cap, daily backups, JSON export.
- Installable PWA, mobile-first.

### Explicitly out of V0.1
Photos/files, the full Family and Us domains (ideas, places, memories, date planning), Life Admin, authenticated calendar APIs, writing to external calendars, email integration, notifications and scheduled Kev runs, custom voice, children as users, native apps, multi-household, embeddings/vector search, project costs, AI design concepts. See ROADMAP.md "Not yet".

## 4. What "done" means

V0.1 is done when both of us can, from a phone:

1. Open Today at 7am and, within about five seconds, understand the day, what needs attention, and whether Kev has noticed anything.
2. Look 30 days ahead and see anything that needs coordinating.
3. Say *"We need to sort the bike"* and have it kept instantly, then approve a sensible task in one tap — or leave it in To sort.
4. Ask *"When could I realistically get that done?"* and get a specific, correct window with a reason.
5. Ask *"What does our weekend look like?"* and get a short, accurate, warm answer, quickly.
6. Tap a child and see their week and the things worth knowing about them.
7. Ask for the Week Ahead and find at least one genuinely useful thing in it.
8. Keep something private from the other person, and have Kev respect it.

## 5. How we will judge it

Run V0.1 for **3–4 weeks** of normal family life.

**Working:** both of us open it most days unprompted; we ask Kev instead of checking several calendars; Kev surfaces a few things per week we'd have missed; at least one home task gets done because Kev found the time; things said to Kev don't get lost.

**Not working:** usage drops after week one; Today isn't trusted; Kev feels generic, wrong or slow; proposals feel like more work than doing it ourselves; To sort becomes a guilt pile.

**Kev quality bar:** zero invented events or times; zero private or sensitive leaks; free-window recommendations always valid; everyday questions answered on the fast tier in a few seconds.

A retrospective at the end decides V0.2 priorities.
