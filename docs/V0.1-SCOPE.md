# HOME V0.1 — Scope

Status: **Proposed — awaiting approval.**

## 1. The one question

> **Does having Kev understand our family context make everyday family coordination meaningfully easier?**

Everything in V0.1 exists to answer that question with real use, by both of us, for a few weeks. Anything that doesn't help answer it is out.

## 2. Challenging the initial hypothesis

The starting hypothesis was: *Today, Forward, Home Projects, Kev — with family members, events, commitments, simple tasks, home projects, notes/photos and Kev contextual awareness.* That is close. Three adjustments:

1. **Real calendar data is essential, not optional.** If events must be typed into HOME by hand, HOME will be incomplete within a week and Kev's answers will be wrong. V0.1 therefore includes **read-only calendar sync (ICS feeds)**. HOME still supports manual events for things that live in nobody's calendar (kids' activities, family plans).
2. **Photos move to V0.2.** Photos add object storage, uploads, signed URLs, image handling and a new privacy surface — and they don't help answer the core question. Projects in V0.1 have tasks and notes. Photos are the first thing added after.
3. **Add the Week Ahead (on demand).** It is the purest test of the hypothesis — Kev reasoning across everything to anticipate the week — and costs almost nothing extra once Today, Forward and Kev exist. No scheduling or notifications; we ask for it.

Also added, because they are cheap now and expensive to retrofit: **visibility (household/private)**, **proposals with approval**, **audit log**, **facts ("What Kev knows")**, and **weather** (needed for the signature "when could I get that done?" moment).

## 3. Recommended V0.1

### Screens
| Screen | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Date and a one-line weather note · each person's day (events, who's doing drop-off/pickup) · conflicts, if any · tasks due or scheduled today · a Kev prompt. Fits on one phone screen in the common case. |
| **Forward** | 7 / 30 / 90-day toggle · grouped by week · events, birthdays, deadlines, scheduled tasks, project target dates · conflicts highlighted quietly · **Week Ahead** button. |
| **Projects** | List of home projects (idea / active / paused / done) · project page with summary, tasks (with estimate + needs), and notes. |
| **Kev** | Conversation available from every screen (sheet/drawer on mobile). Streaming answers, tappable item citations, inline proposal cards with Approve / Edit / Reject. |
| **Settings** | People · Calendar sources · *What Kev knows* (facts) · Activity (audit log) · Export. |

### Capabilities
- Two adult users, magic-link sign-in, allowlist.
- People: adults and children (profiles only).
- Events: manual (with recurrence) + synced from ICS feeds (read-only), with person/responsibility annotations.
- Tasks: simple — title, optional project, assignee, due date, estimate, needs, scheduled window.
- Home projects: title, status, summary, tasks, notes.
- Notes on projects/people/events.
- Facts: Kev-proposed or user-added, approved, editable.
- Deterministic engines: agenda, conflicts, free windows (with daylight and weather).
- Weather for home location.
- Kev: read tools + proposal tools as defined in KEV-AGENT-MODEL.md.
- Week Ahead on demand.
- Visibility: household/private; conversations private per user.
- Audit log; daily backups; JSON export.
- Installable PWA, mobile-first.

### Explicitly out of V0.1
See ROADMAP.md §"Not yet". Headline items: photos/files, Family and Us domains, Life Admin, writing to external calendars, email integration, notifications/push, scheduled Kev runs, children as users, custom voice, native apps, multi-household, vector search/embeddings, cost tracking for projects, AI design concepts.

## 4. What "done" means for V0.1

V0.1 is done when both of us can, from a phone:

1. Open Today and trust it as an accurate picture of the family's day.
2. Look 30 days ahead and see anything that needs coordinating.
3. Say *"Add fixing the garage light to the house"* and approve a correctly-structured task in one tap.
4. Ask *"When could I realistically get that done?"* and get a specific, correct window with a reason.
5. Ask *"What does our weekend look like?"* and get a short, accurate, warm answer.
6. Ask for the Week Ahead and find at least one genuinely useful thing in it.
7. Keep something private from the other person, and have Kev respect it.

## 5. How we will judge it

Run V0.1 for **3–4 weeks** of normal family life.

**Signals that it's working**
- Both of us open it on most days without being reminded.
- We ask Kev things instead of checking multiple calendars or asking each other.
- At least a few moments per week where Kev surfaced something we'd otherwise have missed (clash, pickup gap, good window).
- At least one home task gets done because Kev found the time for it.

**Signals that it isn't**
- We stop opening it after the first week.
- We don't trust Today (missing or wrong items).
- Kev answers feel generic, wrong or slower than just looking.
- Approving proposals feels like more work than doing it ourselves.

**Kev quality bar (from evals and use)**
- Zero invented events or times.
- Zero private-information leaks.
- Free-window recommendations always valid against the engine.

A short retrospective at the end of the trial decides V0.2 priorities.
